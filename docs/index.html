Our approach to AI

We don’t just build AI. We build with it.

Most consultancies treat AI as something to sell. For us it is also how we work. We use AI across our delivery to analyse legacy code, write and review data transformations, generate tests and documentation, and validate data at scale. Experienced engineers still review every line.

So projects move faster, cost less, and end up better tested and better documented than traditional delivery usually allows. And because we use these tools every day, our advice on adopting AI comes from real experience, not slideware.

  • Faster

    AI takes on the repetitive engineering, so our people can focus on design, judgement and your business problem.

  • Higher quality

    Automated testing, documentation and reconciliation are built in from day one, not left to the end.

  • AI-ready by design

    Everything we build is structured, documented and governed, so AI can use your data safely, now and as the technology changes.

02

AI-Driven Data Migration

Move off legacy warehouses, ETL and BI onto the modern data stack, faster and with less risk, with AI doing the heavy lifting.

Leave legacy behind faster, at lower cost and with less risk

Legacy migrations are known for being slow. There are thousands of stored procedures, undocumented ETL jobs, and reports that nobody fully understands. We use AI to do the heavy lifting: mapping your existing estate, translating code to modern tools, generating documentation and tests, and automatically checking that old and new systems produce the same results. Our engineers own the design and review every change. You reach a modern platform sooner and can switch off legacy systems with confidence.

How it works

  1. 1

    Assess. Automated discovery of your estate: code, dependencies, lineage, usage and complexity.

  2. 2

    Plan. Target architecture, migration waves, and a realistic plan based on evidence.

  3. 3

    Convert. AI-assisted translation of SQL, stored procedures, ETL and reports, reviewed by engineers.

  4. 4

    Validate. Automated data reconciliation and parallel running to prove the results match.

  5. 5

    Cut over. A controlled switch-over, user onboarding, and decommissioning of legacy systems.

We commonly migrate from

  • SQL Server (SSIS, SSRS, SSAS)
  • Oracle
  • Teradata
  • Informatica
  • Talend
  • Hadoop
  • SAP BusinessObjects
  • Cognos
  • QlikView
  • Processes built on spreadsheets and Access

Outcomes

  • Shorter migration timelines and lower delivery cost
  • A tested, documented codebase in place of knowledge held in people’s heads
  • Lower licence and infrastructure costs once legacy systems are retired

FAQ

Questions we often hear

What do you mean by “AI-native”?
We use AI throughout our delivery to analyse code, write and test transformations, generate documentation and validate data. Experienced engineers review every output. We also build everything so that your data and systems are ready to be used by AI.
Is AI-generated code reliable?
AI speeds up the work. It doesn’t replace engineering judgement. An engineer reviews every change, and every change is automatically tested and version-controlled, to the same standard as code written by hand.
Do you work with our existing tools and cloud?
Yes. We’re technology-agnostic and work with AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and all major data platforms. We only recommend changes where there’s a clear benefit.
How do you keep our data secure?
Wherever possible we work inside your own environment. We follow least-privilege access and design for UK GDPR compliance and data residency. When we use AI models, we use enterprise services configured so that your data is not used to train them.
How quickly will we see results?
Most engagements begin with a short discovery phase and a focused proof of value. That way you see tangible results early, before committing to a larger investment.
Who owns what you build?
You do. All code, models, documentation and IP created for you belong to you, and we make sure your team can run and extend them.
We’re just starting with AI. Where should we begin?
Start with an AI readiness assessment or a discovery workshop. We’ll find the opportunities with the highest value and lowest risk, and give you a practical roadmap.
