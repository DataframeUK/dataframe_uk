Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Dataframe Limited collects through this website and when you email us, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Dataframe Limited (“Dataframe”, “we”, “us”) is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 13975651. Our registered office is 167–169 Great Portland Street, 5th Floor, London, W1W 5PF.

For any question about this policy or your information, email inquiries@dataframe.uk.

What we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or sign-ups, and it does not ask you for any personal information.

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email includes it, and anything you write or attach.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address so it can run and secure the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Our fonts are served from this website, not from a third party.

How we use your information

We use your email only to reply to you and, if we go on to work together, to manage that relationship. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or taking steps you have asked for before entering into a contract. We will not add you to a mailing list or use your details for marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers who run our email and this website:

  • Microsoft, which provides our email through Microsoft 365
  • GitHub, which hosts this website

We do not sell your personal information.

International transfers

These providers may store or process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge) or the safeguards in the ICO’s International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we start working together, in which case we keep them for as long as our contract and legal obligations require.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it
  • ask us to transfer it to you or another organisation

To use any of these rights, email inquiries@dataframe.uk. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
